PLAYERS ACADEMY  ·  CURATED PLACEMENTS, NOT A JOB BOARD
DEFINITION
— WHAT IS THE PLAYERS ACADEMY?

PARTNERSHIP
PLATFORM.

PROFESSIONAL LEARNING AND
YEAR-ROUND EXPERIENTIAL READINESS

The Business of Sports' dedicated experiential engine. A structured, accountable, credit-bearing program that places students inside Utah's sports industry with vetted employer partners.

PLAYERS ACADEMY  ·  A PARTNERSHIP PLATFORM
WHO BENEFITS  ·  AND HOW
FOR STUDENTS
Curated placements, academic credit, and a clear path from classroom to career in the sports industry.
FOR EMPLOYERS
Access to trained, motivated DESB talent with structured onboarding expectations and defined responsibilities.
FOR THE PROGRAM
Real-world outcomes that strengthen the minor's credibility, donor appeal, and industry reputation.
PLAYERS ACADEMY  ·  THREE AUDIENCES  ·  ONE PROGRAM
PLAYERS ACADEMY  ·  WE WANT YOUR PERSPECTIVE

QUESTIONS.

01Involvement
What would meaningful student involvement practically look like inside your organization: number of students, project duration, mentorship, recruiting access, something else?
02Value
What would make this genuinely valuable enough for your organization to prioritize this partnership?
03Barriers
What barriers (HR, legal, managerial capacity, timing, compensation, student readiness) would we need to solve?
04Relationship
Beyond access to talent, what would you want out of a relationship with the Business of Sports program?
05Ambition
If this became the premier university-to-industry talent platform in sports, what role would you want your organization to play, and what would it take to get there?
06Blind Spots
What are we overlooking that could make this truly distinctive?
