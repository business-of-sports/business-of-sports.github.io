The Case

  • A $500B+ global industry needs cross-functional business and sport talent.
  • Utah's unrivaled ecosystem: pro franchises, Olympic legacy venues, and the 2034 Winter Games.
  • No comparable interdisciplinary sports business minor exists at the University of Utah.
